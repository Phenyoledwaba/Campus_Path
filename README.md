CampusPath: Bridging the Gap between Grade 12 and Graduation

The Problem:
Every year, thousands of South African matriculants pass their exams but fail to enter university. Not because they lack intelligence, but because the application system is confusing, expensive, and inaccessible. Many waste their limited funds on non-refundable application fees for courses they don't qualify for. I know this pain firsthand: it took me four years after my matric to get into university because I didn't know how to navigate the system. I built CampusPath so no learner has to lose years of their life figuring it out.

The Solution:
CampusPath is a robust, enterprise-grade desktop and mobile application designed to simplify higher education access. 

Key features include:
Accurate APS Calculation: Students select their exact NSC subjects, and the app calculates their APS while enforcing strict validation rules (preventing duplicate subjects, requiring languages, and mandating Maths/Maths Literacy).
Real-World Course Matching: Instantly displays qualifying courses for UJ, Wits, and TUT.
Intelligent Funding Engine: Recommends specific funding pathways (NSFAS, ISFAP, Feenix, FNB) based on the student's household income.
Zero-Data USSD Mode: Includes an interactive USSD simulation for students without smartphones or data, addressing a critical gap in the market compared to platforms like UniApplyForMe.

Technologies Used:
Language: Python 3
Frontend UI: Flet (Cross-platform desktop and mobile)
Database: SQLite
Architecture: Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) with modular core and ui packages.
